Welcome to Noor: Zakat & Prayer Tracker ("Noor", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how our app operates. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the rights you have as a user. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Summary — At a Glance

  • Noor is an offline-first application. The vast majority of your data (prayer logs, Zakat calculations, preferences) stays exclusively on your device.
  • We do not operate user accounts, servers, or cloud databases that store your personal information.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • Location and internet access are used only to deliver core features (prayer times, Qibla direction, live gold/silver prices, and ads).
  • You are in full control — uninstalling the app permanently erases all data.

2. Information We Access & Why

2.1 Device Location (Approximate & Precise)

Noor requests access to your device location only to calculate accurate prayer times based on your geographic coordinates and to determine the correct Qibla direction relative to Mecca. Location data is:

  • Processed entirely on-device using the local device sensors and OS APIs.
  • Never transmitted to our servers (we do not operate any).
  • Never shared with third parties for advertising or analytics.
  • Only cached locally to avoid repeatedly requesting GPS access.

You may deny location permission at any time via your device settings. If denied, you can still use the App by manually entering your city.

2.2 Internet Connection

An active internet connection is used only for the following optional functions:

  • Fetching live gold and silver prices for accurate Zakat calculations (public APIs — no personal data is transmitted).
  • Loading advertisements through Google AdMob (see Section 4).
  • Processing in-app purchases and donations through Google Play Billing (see Section 5).
  • Optionally downloading Qur'an audio/text content from public content delivery networks.

2.3 Advertising ID

Google Play Services provides your device with a resettable Advertising ID. Google AdMob may access this ID to deliver relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time under: Device Settings → Google → Ads.

2.4 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device using Android's protected storage:

  • Prayer completion logs and tracker history.
  • Zakat calculation history and financial inputs (all entered voluntarily by you).
  • App preferences, madhab settings, notification preferences, and language.
  • Downloaded Qur'an content and cached prayer time tables.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To be completely clear, Noor does NOT collect, request, or process any of the following:

  • ❌ Name, email, phone number, or user account details.
  • ❌ Contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or calendar data.
  • ❌ Photos, media files, or camera access (unless you use the "share screenshot" feature which uses only OS-level share sheets).
  • ❌ Health, fitness, financial account, or biometric data.
  • ❌ Browsing history from outside the App.

5. In-App Purchases & Donations

Noor offers optional Premium features and voluntary donations through Google Play Billing. All financial transactions are handled by Google — we never receive, view, or store your credit card, bank details, or billing address. Subscription management (cancellation, refunds) is handled through your Google Play account: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

6. Children's Privacy

Noor is intended for a general audience and is safe for users of all ages. It does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. All advertisements are filtered to be family-safe under AdMob's "General Audience" category. The App fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

7. Data Security

Because Noor is offline-first, your data enjoys the strongest possible protection: it never travels over the internet. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's sandboxed app storage and the encryption provided by your device's operating system. We recommend keeping your device's OS up to date and using a device lock (PIN, pattern, or biometrics) for maximum security.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over all data related to the App:

  • Access: All your data is directly viewable inside the App at any time.
  • Correction / Modification: You may edit or update any entry directly within the App.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete all data through any of these methods:
    • Open the App → Settings → "Clear All App Data" button.
    • Device Settings → Apps → Noor → Storage → "Clear Storage".
    • Uninstall the App — this instantly and permanently deletes 100% of app data.
  • Account Deletion: Noor does not use accounts, so there is no server-side profile to delete. The steps above fully satisfy Google Play's account & data deletion requirement.
  • GDPR / CCPA Rights: If you reside in the EU, UK, or California, you have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict, or erase any personal information. Because we do not collect personal data, requests are typically satisfied by the on-device deletion steps above. For further assistance, contact us at the email below.

9. Permissions Summary

  • INTERNET — Load ads, fetch metal prices, process purchases.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Calculate prayer times and Qibla direction.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Send prayer time reminders (optional; you can disable at any time).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Restore scheduled prayer notifications after device restart.
  • VIBRATE — Provide gentle haptic feedback for prayer reminders.
  • WAKE_LOCK — Ensure prayer alarms fire reliably.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — Process Premium purchases and donations via Google Play.
  • AD_ID — Provide anonymous, resettable advertising ID to AdMob.
